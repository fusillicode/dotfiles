agg - Manage local AI agent sessions and maintenance tasks.

USAGE:
    agg
    agg sessions list
    agg sessions list --json --session <agent>:<session-id>...
    agg codex --compact
    agg sync-harness <src_dir> <vendor,vendor,...>
    agg tok [--encoding <name>] <file>
    agg tok [--encoding <name>] --text <text>
    agg tok [--encoding <name>] -

COMMANDS:
    sessions list     Show resumable AI agent sessions.
    codex --compact   Compact the local Codex log database.
    sync-harness      Sync shared AI instructions, skills, and subagent definitions.
    tok               Count text tokens (default encoding: o200k_base).

Run `agg sessions --help`, `agg sessions list --help`, `agg codex --help`,
`agg sync-harness --help`, or `agg tok --help` for command details.

Without a command, shows Claude, Codex, and Cursor resumable sessions in a picker, then resumes or deletes the selected session.
