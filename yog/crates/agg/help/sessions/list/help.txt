List and manage resumable AI agent sessions.

Usage:
  agg sessions list
  agg sessions list --json --session <agent>:<session-id>...

Without --json, open an interactive picker to resume or delete sessions.

Options:
  --json                 Print selected sessions as JSON instead of opening the picker.
  --session <key>        Include a session in JSON output. Repeat for multiple sessions.

The --json form requires at least one --session value.
