agg sync-harness - Sync shared AI configuration to selected vendors.

USAGE:
    agg sync-harness <src_dir> <vendor,vendor,...>

ARGUMENTS:
    src_dir            Source dir containing AGENTS.md, skills/, and subagents/.
    vendor,vendor,...  Comma-separated vendor names: claude, codex, cursor, gemini, opencode.

EXAMPLE:
    agg sync-harness /path/to/ai-config codex,claude,cursor
