agg sync-harness - Sync shared AI configuration to selected agents.

USAGE:
    agg sync-harness <src_dir> <agent,agent,...>

ARGUMENTS:
    src_dir          Source dir containing AGENTS.md, skills/, and subagents/.
    agent,agent,...  Comma-separated agent names: claude, codex, cursor, gemini, opencode.

EXAMPLE:
    agg sync-harness /path/to/ai-config codex,claude,cursor
